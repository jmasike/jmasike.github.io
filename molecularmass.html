Molar mass

Molecular mass is the mass of a single molecule of a substance, whereas molar mass is the mass of one mole 6.02×1023 of particles of that substance.

Molecular Mass vs Molar Mass

Feature Molecular Mass Molar Mass
Definition Mass of 1 individual molecule Mass of 1 mole (6.022 × 1023) of formula units
Scale Microscopic scale Macroscopic (laboratory) scale
Standard Units Atomic mass units (u or amu) or Daltons (Da) Grams per mole (g/mol) or kg/mol
Applicability Restricted to covalent/molecular compounds with discrete molecules Applies to all chemical substances (elements, covalent molecules, ionic compounds, ions)
Measurement Tool Mass spectrometry Electronic laboratory balance (weighed in grams)
