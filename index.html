Hello World! This is my first web page.Here's the short answer: Eating 1 to 2 medium apples a day is a great target for most healthy adults.

Under dietary guidelines (like the USDA’s MyPlate), adults are generally advised to eat **1.5 to 2 cups of fruit per day**. Since one medium apple (about 3 inches in diameter) counts as **1 cup of fruit**, two apples fulfill your entire daily recommended fruit intake.

What 1 Medium Apple (~182g) Provides

* **Calories:** ~95 * **Fiber:** 4.4 grams (*~16% of your daily value*) * **Vitamin C:** 8.4 mg (*~10% of your daily value*) * **Potassium:** 195 mg * **Antioxidants:** Rich in **quercetin** and **catechin**, which support heart health and reduce inflammation.

Key Things to Keep in Mind

* **Pectin & Fiber:** Most of the fiber is soluble fiber called **pectin**, which aids digestion and helps manage cholesterol levels. Keep the peel on—much of the fiber and antioxidants reside in the skin. * **Variety Matters:** While eating an apple every day is beneficial, swapping in other fruits like berries (for extra antioxidants) or citrus (for higher Vitamin C) ensures you get a broader spectrum of micronutrients. * **Whole Fruit vs. Juice:** Stick to whole apples. Apple juice removes the beneficial fiber and concentrated natural sugars (fructose) can lead to quicker blood sugar spikes.
